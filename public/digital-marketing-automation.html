Frequently asked questions

What does your digital marketing service include?

Targeted PPC advertising, multi-channel SEO optimization and growth strategies for major sales events, supported by content, lead capture and reporting.

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Amazon PPC, Flipkart Sponsored Ads, Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram, and Google Ads including Search, Shopping, Performance Max and YouTube Ads.

Can you automate WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger?

Yes. We set up auto-chat workflows for Instagram and Facebook Messenger and integrate the official WhatsApp business messaging API. Automation must follow each platform’s policies and your customers’ opt-in consent. See Meta business solutions for details.

How will I know whether campaigns are working?

We set up tracking such as Meta Pixel and Google Analytics 4, review performance regularly and share monthly growth reports.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. We focus on sound setup, clear measurement and continuous improvement rather than guaranteed outcomes.
