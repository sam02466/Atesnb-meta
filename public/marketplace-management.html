Frequently asked questions

Which marketplaces do you support?

Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, JioMart, Snapdeal, Blinkit and Zepto, with multi-marketplace synchronization so your catalog stays consistent.

What is included in marketplace onboarding?

We handle GST verification, account setup, brand registry, SEO cataloging and shipping template configuration.

How do you protect my seller accounts?

We monitor account health 24/7, resolve policy violations and reactivate suppressed product listings. Proactive monitoring also helps prevent stockouts and suspensions.

Do you guarantee rankings or sales?

No. We focus on sound execution: accurate listings, healthy accounts and well-run advertising. Rankings and sales depend on factors outside anyone’s control, such as competition and marketplace decisions.

Who owns my catalog and account data?

You do. You retain ownership of your e-commerce catalogs and business data. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
