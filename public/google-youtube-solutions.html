Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Rankings depend on many factors outside anyone’s control. We focus on sound on-page foundations, useful content and clear measurement.

Which Google Ads campaign types do you manage?

Search, Shopping, Performance Max and YouTube Ads, planned around your objectives, data and budget.

How do you measure results?

We set up Google Analytics 4 conversion tracking, review performance regularly and report in plain language.

Can you help with my YouTube channel?

Yes. We help organize your channel, improve titles and descriptions, and connect your video content to your business goals.

Do you follow Google’s and YouTube’s policies?

Yes. We follow applicable Google and YouTube policies, including the YouTube Developer Policies and Google API Terms, and ask clients to do the same. See our Terms & Conditions.

Do I need to share my passwords?

No. Sharing login credentials or API secrets is not permitted. Give access through each platform’s supported permission controls.
