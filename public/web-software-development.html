Frequently asked questions

What technologies do you use?

We build on modern web stacks including React, Next.js and Node.js, plus Shopify for storefronts. Mobile apps are built for Android and cross-platform use.

Can you connect my store to the tools I already use?

Yes. We set up payment gateways, CRM systems, third-party logistics and ERP integrations such as Unicommerce, Shiprocket and Tally.

Can you move my existing store to a new platform?

Yes. We handle e-commerce migration and work to preserve your data, design and functionality.

Do you support what you build after launch?

Yes. Analytics, maintenance and support include performance monitoring, security updates and dedicated technical support.

Who owns the finished software?

You retain ownership of your custom source code and business data. Our own platform software and proprietary code remain ours, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.
