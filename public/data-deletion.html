ATES NB ECOMMERCE SOLUTION

Request deletion of your information

If you have contacted ATES NB and want to request deletion of personal information, use the contact process below.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

How to submit a request

Email atesnb1@gmail.com with the subject “Personal Data Deletion Request”. Tell us which email address or contact channel you used and what information you want reviewed.

Verification

To protect your information, we may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity before processing a request. Please do not email passwords, identity document scans or payment credentials unless we have explained a secure and necessary process.

Processing and exceptions

We will review the request and respond within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legal, tax, security, dispute-resolution or legitimate business recordkeeping purposes.

Third-party services

If your information was submitted directly to a third-party service, you may also need to contact that provider to exercise deletion rights under its policy.

Contact

For questions about this process, email atesnb1@gmail.com.
