1Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ATES NB Ecommerce Solutions (“ATES NB”) collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects user data in compliance with applicable laws, including but not limited to Google policies, Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) policies, YouTube API/platform policies, e-commerce compliance standards, and global data protection guidelines.

By accessing or using ATES NB, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you must discontinue use of the platform immediately.

2Scope of Policy

This policy applies to website visitors, registered users, vendors and clients, e-commerce clients, web/app development project stakeholders, API users, and third-party integrations. It covers data collected via the ATES NB platform, website portals, custom e-commerce stores, mobile applications, APIs, and customer communication channels.

3Data We Collect

3.1Personal & Business Information

Name, email address, phone number, business details, billing addresses, and marketplace/store credentials.

3.2Technical & Development Data

IP address, device type, browser information, usage logs, repository data, web/app hosting telemetry, and software performance metrics.

3.3Communication & Social Interaction Data

Messages sent/received via WhatsApp API, Instagram DMs, and Facebook Messenger automation. YouTube channel interaction data, comments, and public engagement metrics processed through authorized API scopes. Campaign data, chat history, and workflow logs (processed but not owned by ATES NB).

3.4Payment & Commercial Data

Transaction details, store sales metrics, and billing information.

Note: We do NOT store card details; payments are handled strictly by secure third-party gateways like Razorpay or Stripe.

4Data Usage

We use collected data for:

  • account creation and authentication
  • delivering omnichannel automation across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and YouTube
  • executing e-commerce management, web/app development, and deployment
  • campaign execution and analytics
  • customer support
  • platform improvement
  • Google partner automation and optimization services delivery
  • legal compliance

5Data Ownership

Users retain ownership of their customer data, e-commerce catalogs, source code, and social assets. ATES NB acts as a data processor, not a data controller. Users are solely responsible for the data they upload, manage, or process.

6Third-Party Services

ATES NB integrates with third-party platforms including Meta APIs, Google Services (Analytics, Ads, YouTube API services), E-commerce marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify, etc.), and payment gateways. Users must comply with respective platform terms of service.

7Platform & Developer Compliance

Users must strictly follow WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, Instagram/Facebook automated messaging rules, YouTube Developer Policies, Google API Terms, and e-commerce marketplace SLAs. Violations may result in account suspension, permanent termination, or reporting to relevant platform authorities.

8Data Security & Storage

We implement secure servers, encryption protocols, and access controls. To ensure system efficiency, chat data, social message history, and temporary logs older than 90 days may be automatically deleted without prior notice. Users are advised to maintain independent backups.

9Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and tracking technologies for analytics (Google Analytics), performance monitoring, marketing optimization services tracking, and enhanced user experience. Users can manage cookies via browser settings.

10Strict No Refund Policy

All payments made to ATES NB for subscription plans, setup services, API usage, e-commerce management, custom web/app development, and digital marketing are final and non-refundable once a service is activated or delivered.

11Limitation of Liability & User Responsibilities

ATES NB shall not be liable for message delivery failures, social platform restrictions, marketplace downtime, custom software bugs introduced externally, or business losses. Users agree to use the platform legally, maintain proper opt-in consent, and avoid spam or prohibited commercial activities.

12Account Suspension, Termination & Changes

ATES NB reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts without notice for policy violations, fraudulent activity, or abuse of services. We may update this policy at any time, and continued use implies acceptance.

Important Notice & Compliance Warning

ATES NB provides official business messaging APIs, e-commerce solutions, and web/app development services in full compliance with Meta, Google, and marketplace guidelines. Prohibited activities including illegal schemes, spam, phishing, scams, counterfeit goods, or unauthorized adult content will result in immediate account termination and reporting to legal authorities without notice.
