Platform access and compliance

We never need your password

Access should be provided through the platform’s supported business access controls. Sharing login credentials or API secrets is not permitted.

Meta sets the rules

Any implementation depends on Meta’s current tools, permissions and policies, which can change. We design around them but cannot control them.

Consent and no spam

You must follow Meta Platform Policies and the WhatsApp Business Policy, maintain proper opt-in consent and avoid unsolicited messages.

Honest expectations

We do not guarantee message delivery speeds or campaign results, and we are not responsible for restrictions imposed by third-party platforms.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need my Facebook or Instagram password?

No. We do not request your account password. Access should be given through Meta’s supported business access controls.

Can you automate Instagram DMs and Messenger?

Yes. We build auto-chat workflows that capture leads instantly from your ad campaigns, within Meta’s automated messaging rules.

Is your WhatsApp integration official?

Yes. We integrate the official WhatsApp business messaging API for secure alerts, broadcast campaigns and customer support.

Do I need my customers’ consent to message them?

Yes. You must maintain proper opt-in consent and avoid spam or prohibited commercial activity. Violations can lead to restrictions from the platforms.

Will you build AI chatbots?

AI chatbot automation for complex conversational sales funnels is on our roadmap as we continue scaling our infrastructure.

How long is chat data kept?

To keep the system efficient, chat data, social message history and temporary logs older than 90 days may be deleted automatically, so we recommend keeping your own backups. See our Privacy Policy.
