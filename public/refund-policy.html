Clear expectations for project changes and cancellations

This page explains the general approach to cancellations and refunds for services provided by ATES NB ecommerce solution.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Project-specific terms

Fees, milestones, payment schedules, cancellation rights and refund conditions should be set out in the written proposal, quotation or service agreement for each project. Where a signed agreement or applicable law provides different rights, that agreement or law will apply.

Work already completed

For services that have started, amounts relating to work completed, committed third-party costs or approved expenses may not be refundable, subject to the agreed terms and applicable law.

Cancellation requests

Please send cancellation requests in writing to atesnb1@gmail.com, including your business name and project reference if available. We will confirm receipt and explain the next steps under the applicable agreement.

Third-party costs

Domain, hosting, advertising spend, software subscriptions, marketplace charges and other third-party fees may be charged directly by those providers and are generally governed by their own refund policies.

Errors or concerns

If you believe you have been billed incorrectly or a deliverable does not match the agreed scope, contact us promptly with the relevant details so the issue can be reviewed.

This general policy is not a substitute for a project contract or legal advice. Final terms should be aligned with the actual services and applicable consumer and contract law.
